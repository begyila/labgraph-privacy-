This Privacy Policy explains how LabGraph Pro handles information when you use its scientific data analysis features on Android.

1. Information handled by the app

LabGraph Pro allows users to select and analyze research datasets, including CSV and XLSX files, and to work with analysis settings, statistical outputs, models, and reports. The contents of those files may include information the user chooses to provide. Users should avoid including personal or confidential information unless appropriate for their own use.

2. How information is used

Selected datasets and analysis settings are used to perform requested calculations, model comparisons, visualizations, and report generation. The app is designed for scientific analysis and includes workflows intended for offline use.

3. Local storage and exported files

The app may keep project data, imported datasets, settings, and outputs on the device as needed for local workflows. Users can export files such as scientific reports and ZIP archives. Exported files are managed by the user and may remain on the device or in destinations selected by the user.

4. Network access and third-party services

LabGraph Pro's scientific analysis workflow is designed to work with datasets selected by the user, including workflows intended for local processing. Files that users choose to export are saved in destinations selected by the user.

Android and Google Play may independently process information in connection with operating-system features, app installation, security, and distribution, under their respective privacy policies. For information about the handling of data in a specific version or feature of LabGraph Pro, please contact the developer at the address below.

If new online features, analytics, or third-party integrations are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the associated information practices.

5. Data retention and deletion

User-controlled exported files can be deleted using the device's file management tools. Locally stored app data can generally be deleted using applicable in-app controls, Android app-data controls, or by uninstalling the app, depending on where files are stored. Exported files saved outside the app may remain after uninstalling.

6. Security

LabGraph Pro is intended to process research information within Android's application environment. The security of information stored or exported on a device also depends on the device's settings, operating system, storage permissions, and the user's own handling of exported files.

7. Children's privacy

LabGraph Pro is designed as a scientific data analysis tool for researchers, students, and other users and is not specifically directed to children. Parents or guardians with privacy-related questions may contact the developer using the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

9. Contact
